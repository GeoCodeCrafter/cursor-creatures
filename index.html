cursor creatures

things that live in your cursor. pick one and move your mouse. they all do something when you click, and most of them do something worse if you stop moving.

lizard

click to feed it. once it's full, don't stop moving.

dragon

click to breathe fire. it burns the headline. hold it and it burns your cursor.

fish

300 of them. click, hold, double click. a shark shows up eventually.

spider

click and it spins a web. then try to fly through it.

snake

stop moving and it strikes. click and it eats its own tail.

centipede

every click splits it in two. don't get to eight.

octopus

steals the letters off the page. then your cursor.

t-rex

roars, jumps, can't reach anything. zip back and forth under it.

ravens

hold still and one lands on your cursor. click for more. move gently.

hands

skeleton arms come up out of the ground. don't fly too low. click for more arms.

chameleon

each eye follows you on its own. click to change its colour. hold still and it shoots its tongue.

jellies

click to add a jellyfish. they drift after you. stop moving and they sting.

moths

they think your cursor is a lamp. click to turn the light off. not too many times.

hydra

click to cut off a head. two grow back. then stand still.

scorpion

click and it stings. hold still and it grabs your cursor.

cat

it chases you. stop and it stalks you. then it pounces.

eel

click to fire a bolt. let it charge to 99 volts and hold still.

mantis shrimp

every punch cracks your screen. hold still for the big one.

crocodile

keep moving. it takes the tail first. the second bite is the death roll.

piranhas

every bite takes a piece of your cursor. then its friends turn up.

owl

its head follows your cursor. go round it. keep going.

hermit crab

click a letter and it tries it on. hold still and it moves into your cursor.

shark

the page is flooding. stay dry. click to bail.

raptor

it's learning to use your cursor. hold still and it takes it. click to make it let go.

pistol shrimp

a claw that snaps louder than a gunshot. hold still for the big one.

spitting cobra

it spits on your screen. click for the wipers. hold still and it goes for your eyes.

komodo dragon

one bite and your cursor starts to lag. click for antidote.

moray eel

stay outside the ring. it has a second set of jaws.

murder hornet

every sting makes your cursor swell. click to swat them.
vanilla js + canvas, no libraries. add ?clean to any of them to hide the text in the corners.
